Residential Treatment Team Meeting (RTTM) GuideRevised 2019

OBH's process for when a residential provider has concerns about admitting someone. A person cannot be declined until an RTTM has been held and recommendations established.

Steps

  1. Request the meeting Within 2 business days of the interview, request an RTTM in the portal. On Placement Outcome, set the interview/tour date and select 'residential treatment team needed'; list the concerns in Referral Notes.
  2. Hold the meeting The provider invites the treatment team and OBH (consumer, SC, CTT, JRS, extended acute units, CIT). The meeting should be held within 3 business days of the request.
  3. Record the decision A decision to admit or decline is expected at the meeting. Enter the outcome on Placement Outcome within one business day; if declined, add a brief description; if admitted, enter the anticipated move-in date.
  4. Submit the guide to OBH Send the completed RTTM guide to obh-centralizedreferrals@alleghenycounty.us within one business day of the meeting.
The form captures applicant, housing level, diagnosis, ADL score, facilitator, attendance, the reason for the RTTM (e.g. arson/assault history, Megan's Law, health/mobility, questionable diagnosis or housing level), and the recommendation/outcome.

Residential Admission ChecklistRevised 2023

The OBH checklist of documentation required to admit someone, with recency rules that vary by housing type.

On this screen

Always required
The Allegheny County Residential Referral form, a signed psychiatric evaluation with current diagnosis, and a signed physical exam (by a physician, not a PA).
Recency varies by program
Psych eval: within 6 months for LTSR; 1 year for CMHPCH, CRR apartment/group home, PCH; 2 years for Supportive Housing. Physical: 6 months for LTSR/CRR/PCH; 2 years for Supportive Housing.
LTSR only
An LTSR Certificate signed/dated by a psychiatrist at referral and again within 30 days before admission; qualification for voluntary (§201) or involuntary (§304/305/306) treatment with a copy of the commitment form; and evidence of severe psychosocial disability from serious mental illness.
CMHPCH & PCH
A doctor-signed MA-51 60 days prior, a Diagnostic Medical Evaluation, a Preadmission Screening Form 30 days prior, and a Personal Care Home Assessment completed 15 days after admission.
