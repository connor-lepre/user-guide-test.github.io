My DashboardReviewed 2024

Lists every referral and case assigned to you, grouped by status, with alerts.

On this screen

Panes by status
Pending, Submitted, Ineligible, and Active (waitlist) referrals; Accepted-Pending (bed matched, pre-move-in); Active Cases (in a bed); and any Shared referrals or cases. Empty panes are hidden.
Alerts
Alerts appear when a referral is accepted or sent to a provider, when an accepted-pending referral has no interview after 3 days or no move-in date after 7 days, or when a move-in date has expired. Alerts are removed automatically once resolved.
Find and sort
Sort by any column, paginate, and use the per-grid search to filter by name, date, or number. Supervisors and DHS staff can switch the Assigned Worker dropdown to view other workers' dashboards.

Advanced SearchReviewed 2023

Search all referrals and cases you have access to, using multiple criteria.

On this screen

Search criteria
Combine entity type (All / Referral / Case), entity or client ID, client name, SSN, and a date range. Filter cases by Active/Discharged, or filter by one or more referral statuses.
Results
Use the Search-within field to narrow the results grid, then click an Entity ID to open it.
