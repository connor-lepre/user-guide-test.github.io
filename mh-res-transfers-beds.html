Transfer a consumer to another bedReviewed 2018

Create a new referral for a consumer already on your active caseload so they can move to a different bed. Transfers start from the consumer's Case Summary.

Steps

  1. Start from Case Summary Open the case, go to Case Summary, and select Transfer. Complete the Transfer Referral Details pop-up (reason for transfer, description) and Save.
  2. Complete the new referral Synergy creates a new referral (it appears under Pending Referrals on your dashboard) and opens its Individual Info screen. Case information carries over. Complete every required screen and Submit.
  3. Close the old case Once the consumer is accepted into the new bed placement, close the current case.
The transfer uses the full referral workflow. See 'Create & complete a referral' for each screen.

Bed swapReviewed 2018

Move an individual between beds and notify DHS. A Residential Site Supervisor or Manager starts the swap from the Housing Inventory screen.

Steps

  1. Open the room in Housing Inventory From Manage Housing Inventory, find the facility, select the individual's room, and choose Bed Swap.
  2. Request the move In the pop-up, pick the destination room, enter the reason in Notes, and send the request.

Troubleshooting

What to do
Both beds are occupiedRecoverable The swap is approved automatically, and DHS is notified by email.
Moving someone into a vacant bedCheck DHS reviews the request manually. If it is approved, the case is updated with the new bed. Check Synergy before contacting DHS.
The request was rejectedCheck You are notified only if the request is rejected. Open the Rejected Bed Swap pane on your dashboard to resubmit with more detail, or clear the notice.

Placement outcomeReviewed 2017

Placement provider staff document the treatment-team meeting, the consumer's tour and interview, and the final placement outcome.

On this screen

Residential Treatment Team Meeting
Log each meeting: date, worker, notes, and any additional supports the person would need to live there successfully.
Tour and interview
Scheduled and actual interview/tour dates. You must either give a scheduled tour date or a reason it wasn't scheduled.
Outcome
Record the meeting and tour/interview outcomes (at least one required), reason for declination if declined, and any admission delays. If the consumer is accepted, also record the anticipated and actual move-in dates and the expected discharge date.
These dates control dashboard alerts. The referral is flagged if there is no interview after 3 days or no move-in date after 7 days.

Housing inventory (facilities & rooms)Reviewed 2017

Update facility details and manage rooms. Bed swaps also start from this screen.

On this screen

Facility information
Edit contact person, phone/fax, level of care, and accessibility (wheelchair, hearing, vision). Bed and room counts are read-only and are calculated from the rooms below.
Room information
Add or edit rooms: name (permanent once saved), type, floor, service, gender, handicapped bathroom, reconfigurable, lease holder, and room status.
New facilities must be added in MPER first; they cannot be created here. Suspensions are documented from the individual's case (Individual Info → View/Add Suspension Details), not here.
