Workflow rules

Applies to every screen below
  • Save before you leave a screenUnsaved changes are lost when you move away, especially with Back.
  • Back goes to the DashboardUsing it before a new referral's first save starts the referral over.
  • Mandatory screens are flaggedTheir marker in the left navigation changes to a completed icon once saved.

1

Start from the ACDHS Portal (housing eligibility)

Reviewed 2017

Referrals begin in the ACDHS Portal. Staff enter a consumer's details to check eligibility across housing programs, then start an LTSR referral in MH Residential.

Steps

  1. Check eligibility In the Housing Eligibility Calculator, enter date of birth, current residence type, household size, income, disability, insurance, military service, and whether onsite support is needed.
  2. Review & compare programs Review the eligibility results; add programs to Compare to see them side by side with descriptions, who they serve, services, and contacts.
  3. Start the referral If MH Residential is in the results, select Start Referral (or Continue with LTSR Referral on the Housing Eligibility tile). Enter Consumer Information and Save and Next.
  4. Client Search Synergy checks for an existing referral. If one exists, a new referral can't be created; otherwise select Create New Referral to continue into MH Residential's Individual Info screen.
Details entered here (name, DOB, legal sex, insurance, veteran status) carry over into the referral's Individual Info screen.

2

Individual info

Reviewed 2023

The first screen of a new referral. It records the consumer's demographics, SOGIE information, address(es), and representative payee, guardianship, and power-of-attorney details. The consumer is MCI-cleared and the referral is created on this screen.

Steps

  1. Start from the ACDHS Portal A referral usually begins from the Portal's Eligibility Results (Start Referral) or the Housing Eligibility tile (Continue with LTSR Referral). Name, DOB, legal sex, insurance and veteran status carry over automatically.
  2. Complete Individual Details Enter name, SSN, birth date, legal sex, race, source of income, health insurance, SOAR and veteran status.
  3. MCI-clear the consumer Compare your entry against potential matches. A match of 95% or higher must be selected; Synergy does not allow a new MCI ID when one exists. Create a new ID only when there is no match.
  4. Complete the Referral Summary Set referral date, priority category, provider, facility/unit, assigned worker, readiness level (and date if 'Ready in 90 days'), and mental-health diagnoses. Saving here creates the referral and assigns its ID.
  5. Add SOGIE, address, and payee/guardian info Document SOGIE (or check Did Not Ask Yet), add and validate address(es) against Postal, and record representative payee, guardian, and power-of-attorney details.

Troubleshooting

What to do
Left the screen before MCI-clearing and completing the Referral SummaryCheck Both are required before a new referral can be saved. Complete them before leaving the screen, or the entry is lost.
An address won't validate against PostalCheck If the address is correct, use Create address as entered to save it without validation.
Potential match is 95%+ but you believe it's a different personCheck You must select the match to clear the consumer. If it is a different person, contact the Service Desk. Synergy does not create a new ID when a 95%+ match exists.
Each referral has one consumer. After a consumer is entered, the New button is disabled.

3

Referral information

Reviewed 2017

Records who is making the referral (the Referral Source) and the consumer's Service Support information.

On this screen

Referral Source
Check Self? to auto-fill your own contact info. Capture name, office/cell phone, email, and the source's supervisor. Note whether MH housing has been discussed with the Service Support Program, and the Referral Provider type.
Service Support
Check Same as above if the support contact matches the source. Record whether the consumer has a Service Support; if yes, capture the program and team, provider agency, worker contact, recent CANS/ANSA date, and supervisor.
No Service Support
If the consumer has none, record whether a support referral was made and its date, the program type and provider, and a contact person. If no referral was made, an explanation is required.

4

Residential living

Reviewed 2017

Documents the consumer's prior residential-living history (last 5 years), other housing options being explored, and the housing programs they were found eligible for in the ACDHS Portal.

On this screen

Residential living experience
If the consumer lived in a residential facility in the last 5 years, record each: facility type, a narrative of the experience, program name, dates, and outcome.
Other housing options
Whether other options are being explored, selected from a multi-select list.
Eligible housing programs
Read-only, carried over from the Portal eligibility check.

5

Treatment history

Reviewed 2023

Documents the consumer's current mental-health diagnosis and their most recent psychiatric evaluation.

On this screen

Psychiatric evaluation
Date, evaluator, and evaluating agency of the most recent evaluation.
Mental health diagnosis
Add each diagnosis: diagnostic system, appointment date, priority group, axis/category and sub-category, primary/secondary, and description or code (entering one fills in the other).
Eligibility: the consumer must have a primary diagnosis of a Serious Mental Illness within the past 12 months. A primary diagnosis of alcohol or substance use, an organic brain syndrome, a developmental disability, or a social condition does not qualify.

6

Risk factors

Reviewed 2023

Documents risk factors to consider in the housing search, plus any substance-use history.

On this screen

Risk factors
Yes/No for each: suicidal ideation, homicidality, harm to others, property destruction, fire safety, trauma/abuse history, reckless behavior, Megan's Law, weapons access, medical risk. A 'Yes' opens a required explanation.
Substance use history
Whether the consumer is currently using drugs or alcohol, and whether they have a history of substance use.
Use the History icon in the pane's title bar to see prior versions of what was saved.

8

Level of care

Reviewed 2023

Records the level of care the consumer needs, plus housing needs, special accommodations, and any physical-health conditions that affect housing. The level of care entered here determines which authorization screen comes next.

On this screen

Level of care information
Primary level of care (from the Referral Summary), site preference, and whether a private room is needed.
Housing needs
Check any needs/preferences; specify a town or 'Other'.
Special accommodations
Mobility needs, wheelchair/walker, whether a 1st-floor location is required, with details for each.
Physical health
Whether the consumer has physical-health conditions that impact housing, and which.
Must be completed before Applicant Authorization or LTSR Acknowledgement. If the level of care is LTSR, go to LTSR Acknowledgement next; if it isn't, go to Applicant Authorization.

9

Applicant authorization

Reviewed 2017

Captures the consumer's authorization for the referral, for non-LTSR levels of care. Complete Level of Care first.

On this screen

Applicant authorization
Review the authorization statement with the consumer and check the agreement box. The applicant signature cannot be captured until the box is checked.
Signatures
Capture a digital signature (touchscreen + stylus) for both the applicant and the referral source, or select paper signature and upload it later to the Document Folder.
Once saved, Submit becomes active. Use Chrome or Internet Explorer. Synergy is not compatible with Edge. If paper signatures were used, scan and upload them before submitting.

10

LTSR acknowledgement

Reviewed 2017

Captures the consumer's acknowledgement of the referral, for the LTSR level of care. Complete Level of Care first.

On this screen

LTSR acknowledgement
Review the acknowledgement statement with the consumer. If they're unable or refuse to sign, check that box and explain why.
Signatures
Capture a digital or paper signature for both the applicant and the referral source; the applicant may also be marked Refused to sign.
Once saved, Submit becomes active. Use Chrome or Internet Explorer. Synergy is not compatible with Edge. Upload any paper signatures before submitting.

Assessment history

13

Share the referral & exchange provider feedback

Reviewed 2018

Share view or edit access to a referral with another MH Residential user, and exchange feedback between DHS and residential providers about a prospective referral.

On this screen

Referral sharing
Pick an agency/provider, unit, and staff member, then grant view or write access per screen (write implies view). Sharing any screen automatically shares Individual Info. Shared referrals appear on the recipient's dashboard; supervisors can manage sharing for their unit.
Residential provider feedback
DHS can request feedback from multiple providers, who get read-only access to review the referral and add feedback notes. All participants can see feedback from DHS and from each provider.
