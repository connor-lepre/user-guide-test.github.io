MH Residential

Referrals, placement and case management for mental-health residential housing. Guides are organized by task.

Tool
MH Residential
Access
Sign in through Okta. A supervisor or agency designee requests access with the ISAR form; the Service Desk manages credentials.
Last reviewed
2017–2024
Status
Most guides date from 2017–2019 and may show older screens or an Internet Explorer / Chrome note.
Use for
Referrals into mental-health residential housing, bed placement and transfers, and the active case through discharge.
Users
Residential providers, site supervisors and managers, OBH / DHS staff
Similar tools
  • HMIS(opens in a new tab) Housing enrollments, referrals, units and reporting.
  • Dragonfly(opens in a new tab) Consumer info, referrals and technical-assistance tracking.
