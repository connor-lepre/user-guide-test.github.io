What MH Residential does

It runs the full path into mental-health residential housing: a provider checks a consumer's housing eligibility in the ACDHS Portal, builds a referral, submits it to DHS, and — once the consumer is placed in a bed — manages the ongoing case, transfers, and discharge.

Who it helps

  • Residential providers creating and completing referrals.
  • Site supervisors & managers assigning work, sharing, and managing beds.
  • OBH / DHS staff reviewing referrals and bed moves.

How to get access

Sign in through Okta. Access is requested by a supervisor or agency designee through the ISAR form; the Service Desk manages credentials.

Most of these guides were written between 2017 and 2019 and may show older screens or an Internet Explorer / Chrome note — access is now through Okta. That drift is why the guides are flagged “needs review.”

How a referral moves

Pending→Submitted→Active (waitlist)→Accepted-pending (bed matched)→Active case→Discharged
