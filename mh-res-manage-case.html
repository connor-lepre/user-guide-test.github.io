Manage an active case

After a consumer is placed, the referral becomes a case, managed from the Case Summary screen.

The case reuses the referral screens. Individual Info, Referral Info, Treatment History, Risk Factors and Legal History look the same on a case as on a referral and are documented on the Create & complete a referral page. This page covers the two screens specific to cases.
Case SummaryReviewed 2017

The main screen of an active case. It shows placement and status information. Supervisors use it to edit the case, start a transfer, record suspensions, and discharge the consumer.

On this screen

Case Summary pane
Placement date, case status, provider agency, assigned worker, priority category, estimated discharge date, and bed information. Supervisors and managers can edit here.
Next steps
Jump to the consumer's Individual Info, or view all referrals linked to the case.
Suspensions
Use View/Add Suspension Details to record a bed suspension's start/end dates and reason.

Troubleshooting

What to do
Consumer needs to move to a different level of careRecoverable Use Transfer. It creates a new referral under Pending Referrals with the case data carried over. Close the case after the consumer is accepted into the new bed.
Consumer has been dischargedCheck Set an estimated discharge date, change Case Status to Discharged, then complete the Case Discharge Details (date, reason, notes) to close the case.
